## 3. Detailed Description / 详细描述

### English Version

**Transform your local PDF collection into a powerful, private library directly in your browser.**

PDF Library is designed for students, researchers, and developers who manage strictly local PDF files and care about privacy. Unlike other extensions, we **never** upload your files to the cloud. Everything stays on your machine.

**Key Features:**

*   **📚 Visual Bookshelf:** Say goodbye to messy file explorers. View your local PDF folders as a clean, organized grid.
*   **⏳ Auto-Save Progress:** Never lose your place again. The extension automatically remembers the exact page where you left off for every single file.
*   **🔒 100% Private & Offline:** Built with the modern File System Access API. Your data lives on your hard drive, not our servers.
*   **⚡ Fast & Lightweight:** A custom reader interface focused on speed and reading comfort.
*   **📂 Easy Management:** Add multiple directories from different locations on your computer to build a unified library.

**How it works:**
1. Click "Add Directory" to select a folder from your computer.
2. Grant permission to access the files.
3. Your PDFs appear instantly in the library.
4. Click to read!

**Permissions:**
- `Storage`: Used to save your reading progress (page numbers) locally.

### Chinese Version (中文版)

**将您的本地 PDF 收藏转化为浏览器中强大、私密的个人图书馆。**

PDF Library 专为需要管理本地 PDF 文件的学生、研究人员和开发者设计。与市面上的其他工具不同，我们**绝不**会将您的文件上传到云端。所有数据都安全地保留在您的设备上。

**核心功能：**

*   **📚 可视化书架：** 告别杂乱的文件管理器。以整洁、直观的网格视图浏览您的本地 PDF 文件夹。
*   **⏳ 自动记忆进度：** 再也不用担心忘记读到哪里了。扩展程序会自动记录每一个文件的阅读位置，下次打开即刻续读。
*   **🔒 100% 隐私与离线：** 基于现代化的 File System Access API 构建。您的文件只在本地运行，无需互联网连接。
*   **⚡ 极速轻量：** 专为阅读舒适度打造的自定义阅读器，响应迅速。
*   **📂 轻松管理：** 支持添加计算机上不同位置的多个文件夹，构建统一的知识库。

**使用方法：**
1. 点击“添加文件夹 (Add Directory)”选择您电脑上的 PDF 目录。
2. 授权访问文件（仅限本地读取）。
3. 您的 PDF 将立即出现在书架上。
4. 点击封面即可开始阅读！

**权限说明：**
- `Storage`：仅用于在本地保存您的阅读进度（页码）。
